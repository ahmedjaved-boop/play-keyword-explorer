# App Package
Enter a keyword and press Search. Results are the live top 200 for that country, in Google Play’s rank order.
Source: live Google Play search (play.google.com/store/search) read by this site’s own backend; Play caps search results at about 250. Installs, ratings and update dates are not in search results, so “Enrich” fetches each app’s store page (top 30 at a time, 10 in parallel). Results are cached for 10 minutes to stay under Google’s rate limits.
